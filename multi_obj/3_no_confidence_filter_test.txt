Current filter: a candidate (activity, resource) enters the Pareto front only if
    P(outcome with > outcome without) > 0.5  OR  P(remaining time with < without) > 0.5.
Without the filter: thresholds -1, every candidate passes. Everything else unchanged: window 5, k = 5,
min_next_share 0.01, min_res_share 0.05, prosit with the cached parameters, setting B, 10 runs, step 9 with
x = 5, y = 10. The baseline does not depend on the filter: the current baseline runs were reused.

Control: step 3 rerun WITH the filter gives the same recommended points as the files on disk (same set of points
for every prefix; 3 prefixes of bpi17_after differ, ties in the top-k selection). The order of the k points over
the ranks is not stable between runs (p-dispersion): it does not matter here, step 9 pools the ranks.

- The new prefixes (+5 / +22 / +50 / +38) are all prefixes where NO candidate is likely to beat "no

- So in practice the filter decides WHEN NOT TO RECOMMEND, plus a few extra points in prefixes that already have
  a recommendation (BPI12: 60 prefixes). Most candidates it would discard are already dominated on the front.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. CONCLUSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
With the thresholds at 0.5 the confidence filter has little influence on the step-9 metrics: it removes 0.2-8.8%
of the points, all estimated not to improve either KPI, so the agreement figures move within the noise. What it
does is decide when not to recommend anything (5 / 22 / 50 / 38 prefixes left without recommendation, 0.3-4.1%
of the test prefixes) and drop the clearly harmful candidates: on BPI12 the removed points are confirmed as harmful
by the simulation (-51 pp of outcome, +33% of time), on bpi17 they are only mildly worse. A stricter threshold
(see 3_confidence_filter_sweep.txt) would have a larger effect; this test only measures the current one against no
filter.
